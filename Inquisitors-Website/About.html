About Us

The INQUISITORS AI & Quiz Society is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting Artificial Intelligence, innovation, research, and competitive learning. The society provides an excellent platform for students to improve technical knowledge while developing leadership and communication skills.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To create an inclusive, competitive, and collaborative space where students can sharpen their analytical thinking through quizzing and apply that same rigor to solving problems using artificial intelligence.

Our Vision

To become the leading student society bridging traditional knowledge competitions with next-generation technology skills, producing well-rounded thinkers ready for both the quiz stage and the tech industry.

Core Values

  • Curiosity First - We reward good questions as much as good answers.
  • Inclusive Learning - No prior AI or quizzing experience required to join.
  • Hands-On Building - We prefer shipped projects over unfinished theory.
  • Healthy Competition - We compete hard and support each other harder.
