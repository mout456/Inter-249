Welcome

Welcome to the INQUISITORS AI & Quiz Society. We are a community of curious learners passionate about Artificial Intelligence, innovation, problem-solving, and quiz competitions. Our society provides opportunities for students to develop technical knowledge, leadership skills, and teamwork through engaging events and projects.

Mission

Our mission is to promote AI education, encourage critical thinking, and create a collaborative environment where students can learn emerging technologies while improving their analytical and communication skills.
