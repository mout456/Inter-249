Our Projects

Every semester, teams within Inquisitors design and build applied AI projects, many of which grow out of ideas first tested at our quiz and hackathon events. Below are some of our featured projects.

Featured Projects

QuizBot AI

An AI-powered chatbot that generates and grades quiz questions in real time across multiple categories, used during our internal practice rounds.
Tech Used: Python, Natural Language Processing, Flask
Status: Completed

Smart Attendance System

A facial-recognition based attendance system built for society meetings and workshops, reducing manual sign-in time by over 80%.
Tech Used: Python, OpenCV, SQLite
Status: Deployed and in active use

Quiz Ranking Predictor

A machine learning model that analyzes past quiz performance data to predict team rankings and suggest practice focus areas ahead of major competitions.
Tech Used: Python, Scikit-learn, Pandas
Status: In active development

Inquisitors Community Portal

An internal web portal for members to register for events, submit quiz answers, and track their participation history.
Tech Used: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js
Status: Completed
